# Blueprint demo and case-study recording cues

Target length: 7–9 minutes. Record the product walkthrough first, then open the Case Study from the same app.

## Part I — Product demo

1. **Opening (0:00–0:25)** — Show the landing page. Frame Blueprint as an evidence-first decision system, not another idea generator.
2. **Landing promise (0:25–0:50)** — Point to “Turn the unfinished idea into the next provable move.” Briefly explain the uncertainty-to-evidence motion.
3. **Idea entry (0:50–1:10)** — Enter one online-product idea in “What are you trying to make real?” and select “BUILD MY BLUEPRINT →”.
4. **Onboarding (1:10–1:55)** — Move through the eight numbered questions. Mention audience, goal, current position, resources, prior action, and constraints.
5. **Generation (1:55–2:10)** — Pause on “Building your Blueprint” and explain that the app combines user context with planning priors.
6. **Dashboard orientation (2:10–2:50)** — Show Roadmap & Progress, the four headline KPIs, Your starting position, and the phase navigation.
7. **Executable action (2:50–3:35)** — Open Foundation and expand “Define the problem hypothesis”. Show WHAT YOU WILL DO, WHY IT MATTERS, FRAMEWORK & LEARNING, and DONE WHEN.
8. **Working state (3:35–3:55)** — Select “Mark complete”. Show the action becoming “Completed” and the Foundation counter updating.
9. **Decision support (3:55–4:25)** — Show Quick note, Financial plan, and Key signals. Explain the difference between inputs, planning priors, and observed evidence.
10. **Full Blueprint (4:25–5:00)** — Select “VIEW SYSTEM MAP →”. Show the connected phases, action branches, dependencies, decision gates, and export option.

## Part II — Case-study story

11. **Problem and thesis (5:00–5:45)** — Open Case Study. Cover sections 01 and 02: building became faster, but knowing what to build did not; Blueprint supports the 0 → 10 → 100 journey.
12. **Experience and data (5:45–6:40)** — Cover user goals, the evolution of the experience, and the Capture → Normalize → Match → Personalize → Present pipeline.
13. **Architecture and product rules (6:40–7:35)** — Cover information architecture, provenance, Vibe Coding rules, and the proof-centered product loop.
14. **Future and close (7:35–8:10)** — Cover future iterations and reflection. End on “Bring the unfinished version. Blueprint will show what it needs to earn next.”

## Recording checks

- Use one online-product example throughout.
- Do not describe planning priors as market evidence.
- Show at least one completion interaction.
- Keep the Full Blueprint visible long enough to understand the system map.
- Avoid scrolling while delivering the most important sentence in each section.
- Confirm no API key, local path, or private configuration is visible.
